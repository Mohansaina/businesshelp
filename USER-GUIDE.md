# User Guide

Welcome to the AI Review & Reputation Tool! This comprehensive guide will help you get started and make the most of our platform.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Visit the application homepage
2. Click on "Sign Up" or "Get Started"
3. Fill in your:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
4. Click "Create Account"
5. You'll receive a welcome email with next steps

### Logging In

1. Visit the application homepage
2. Click on "Log In"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Click "Sign In"
5. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Social Login

You can also log in using:
- Google Account
- GitHub Account

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't log in**
- Check email and password
- Reset password if needed
- Try social login options

**Not receiving emails**
- Check spam/junk folder
- Verify email address
- Contact support

**Reviews not appearing**
- Check platform connections
- Refresh data manually
- Verify account permissions

### Contact Support
